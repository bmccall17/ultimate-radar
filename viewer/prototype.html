What this prototype is for. It settles the viewer — what a coach sees, how confidence is shown, how a mistake gets fixed — before a line of pipeline code is written. The camera view is a real pinhole projection of the synthetic positions through a simulated broadcast camera, so the overlay and the overhead view are genuinely two renderings of the same geometry, the way they will be with real footage.

Read the shading, not just the dots. A solid dot is a player the camera can see right now. A dashed dot is the system’s guess, and the faint ring around it is how wrong that guess could be. The dashed box on the overhead view is the slice of field the camera is actually pointed at. Anything outside it is inference, and the readouts above say so.
