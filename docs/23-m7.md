# 23 — M7: the second possession, and what it cost

The running list M7 asks for: **every constant, threshold or assumption that had to be
touched to run a second possession**, what broke, what it became, and how it was decided.

The rule this document is written under: **no special-casing p0003.** A constant is either
measured per possession, derived from something already in `clip.json` / `calibration.json`,
or recorded here as an honest manual step. A silent hand-edit is the thing `HANDOFF` § 6
exists to prevent.

---

## The clip

`p0003` was re-cut before anything else, for the reason in `docs/22-m7-preflight.md`: the old
one spanned a camera cut at +19.6 s and six seconds of celebration close-up.

Re-cut to **1580.0 s + 37.0 s** rather than docs/22's suggested 1598.0 + 19.4, after checking
the headroom the way docs/22 asked:

| | evidence |
|---|---|
| one shot? | `eval/m0/cuts_full` has scene candidates at **1560.542** and **1617.599** and nothing between. The tightening of framing near the end is a zoom, not a cut. |
| one possession? | a Sol player holds the disc at 1586, 1594, 1604 and 1611; score ATX 3 – MIN 5 throughout; game clock runs 4:27 → 3:50 without stopping. No turnover. |
| where the cut lands | frames at 18.67 / 19.20 / 19.40 s are live field; **19.60 s is already the close-up**. The cut is exactly where the 0.2347 scene score said. |
| margin | the clip ends at 1617.0, **0.6 s** before the boundary. |

37.0 s, 555 frames. Longer than p0001's 24.0 s / 360 frames deliberately: a longer pan is a
harder test, and it turned out to be the thing that broke M1.

Starting at 1580 rather than 1572 skips the pull. The pull is framed so wide that players are
a few pixels tall, and failing there would have been a detection-scale failure wearing the
costume of a generality failure.

## 1. The camera-centre bundle — **fixed, and it was hiding in p0001 too**

**What broke.** `python -m ur.calibrate.run work/p0003` raised
`RuntimeError: no confident frames with paint; cannot build the map`, with **0 %** of 555
frames at confidence ≥ 0.5 and a median residual of 0.0000 yd — the collapsed-fit signature.

**Where.** Not the framing, which was the obvious suspect and is wrong. Measured on every 15th
frame, p0003 is **not** feature-poor — it is slightly *richer* than p0001:

| | p0003 | p0001 |
|---|---|---|
| centre circle found | 36/37 (97 %) | 24/24 (100 %) |
| halfway line found | 25/37 (67 %) | 14/24 (58 %) |
| **both**, which is what the fit needs | **24/37 (64 %)** | 14/24 (58 %) |

The failure is `fit.bundle_world`. The anchor solved cleanly at frame 260 —
`C = (-19.5, -41.25, 6.0)`, rms 0.2304 yd. The bundle then moved the centre to
**`(-11.6, -24.2, 3.5)`**: three metres above the ground and *inside the pitch*, the near
sideline being at soccer y = −32.75. Every frame after it collapsed.

**Why no local check catches it.** The ten bundle probe frames each fitted at rms 0.11–0.42 yd
with healthy line support, and all ten were accepted. The inputs look fine, the optimiser
converges, and it returns a camera. `least_squares` starts at the anchor's centre and only
accepts improvements, so the bundle is always better *on its own objective* — the objective is
just built on observations associated under poses that are quietly wrong. This is `HANDOFF` § 8
for the tenth time: **something reports success while being wrong.**

**The fix, and why it is not a constant.** Solve the whole possession twice — once with the
bundled centre, once with the anchor's own — and keep whichever the frames prefer. There is no
threshold: it is an **argmax over two candidates**.

Mean per-frame confidence is the score, for two reasons. It is already the project's own
measure of a trustworthy frame (AD-1), so nothing new is invented. And it is immune to the way
a collapsed fit flatters itself: `confidence_of` returns 0.0 for a fit `_summarise` rejects, so
rms 0.0000 scores nothing instead of scoring perfectly. **Median rms would have chosen the
collapsed answer.**

| | bundled | anchor-only | chosen |
|---|---|---|---|
| **p0001** mean confidence | **0.6940** | 0.5909 | bundled |
| **p0001** frames ≥ 0.5 | 93.9 % | 93.9 % | — a tie, so coverage alone could not decide |
| **p0001** median fit rms | 0.1490 yd | 0.2286 yd | |
| **p0001** venue residual | 0.0 yd | 2.7 yd | |
| **p0003** mean confidence | 0.0000 | **0.4177** | anchor |
| **p0003** frames ≥ 0.5 | 0 % | 53.3 % | |

So the bundle genuinely helps p0001 and genuinely destroys p0003, and one rule with no tuning
gets both right. The decision and both candidates are written into `calibration.json` under
`method.centre_choice`, so it is auditable rather than merely printed.

**A second thing this bought.** `_summarise` has always computed *why* a fit is not `ok`, and
the run threw that string away. A possession scoring 0.00 everywhere gave no clue which of the
four failures it was. It is now printed and stored per frame, which is how the arc-span versus
collapse distinction above was read off at all.

**Left undone, deliberately.** The root cause is that every bundle probe frame is initialised
from the *anchor's* pose, which is a poor guess 290 frames away across a long pan. Chaining the
initialisation outward from the anchor is the fix to the cause rather than the symptom, and is
a few lines. It is not in this change because changing two things at once would leave neither
measured. The acceptance rule is a safety net that should stay regardless.

## 2. The documented recipe is missing two steps — **manual step, recorded**

`docs/22`, `HANDOFF` and the M7 brief all give the run as four commands: `ingest`,
`calibrate.run`, `detect.run`, `track.run`, `make_view`. Run exactly that and it fails twice:

- after `detect.run`, **every detection has `team: null`** and `detections.json` records
  `"team_assignment": "not done here - M3 fills team and team_score"`. `python -m ur.team` is
  a separate command.
- `make_view` raises `FileNotFoundError: work\p0003\possession.json`. `python -m ur.possess`
  is a separate command.

The working order is:

```powershell
python -m ur.ingest ...
python -m ur.calibrate.run work\p0003
python -m ur.detect.run    work\p0003
python -m ur.team          work\p0003     # <- missing from every written recipe
python -m ur.track.run     work\p0003
python -m ur.possess       work\p0003     # <- missing from every written recipe
python -m ur.issues        work\p0003     # optional; fills the "needs a human" queue
python -m tools.make_view  work\p0003
```

Nothing was changed to fix this — it is a documentation defect, not a code one, and both
failures are loud. Recorded here rather than silently worked around.

## 3. The viewer quoted p0001's accuracy over p0003's footage — **fixed**

The provenance banner on p0003 read:

> Real footage. youtube IDnoyd4cKfM from 1580.0s, 555 frames at 15 fps. **Tracker recall 88 %
> of player-frames, sigma containment 80 % (docs/17).**

Neither number has ever been measured on p0003. They are **hardcoded literals in
`ur/possess.py`**, measured once against hand labels that exist only for p0001, and emitted
into every possession's `possession.json` as though they were its own.

This is the thing `docs/05` exists to forbid, aimed at the user rather than at a log: a coach
reads that as "this tracker has been checked on the footage I am looking at".

**Fixed** by naming the possession the numbers came from — `gates.measured_on` — and having
the viewer compare it with the possession on screen. p0003 now reads:

> **Accuracy on this possession is unmeasured.** Recall 88 % of player-frames and sigma
> containment 80 % were measured on `p0001` (docs/17), against hand labels that do not exist
> for this one. Treat them as a prior, not a result.

p0001 is unchanged. This generalises for free to a whole game.

## 4. The viewer's own self-check passed on a collapsed homography — **fixed**

The orientation self-check added in this session (step 0) passed on p0003 — at frame 56, with
an image delta of **1.2 × 10¹¹ pixels**. That frame's calibration has collapsed; the sign
comparison was luck.

A check that can pass on nonsense is not a check. It now skips frames the calibration itself
does not stand behind (confidence < 0.5), rejects projections outside ±10 frame-widths, and
requires **every** usable frame to agree rather than the first one it finds. On p0003 it now
reports `checked: 25, degenerate: 0, disagreed: 0`; on p0001, `checked: 25, disagreed: 0`.

Found only because p0003 was run. This is the value of a second possession in one line.

## 5. `--eval-dir` defaults to one directory for every possession — **recorded, not fixed**

`ur.calibrate.run --eval-dir` defaults to `eval/m1` whatever possession it is given. Running it
on p0003 **overwrote `eval/m1/calib_state.npz` and `eval/m1/paint_map.png`**, which are p0001's
committed M1 acceptance evidence. Restored from git; p0003's went to `eval/m1-p0003`.

Nothing warned. A second possession silently destroys the first one's evidence, and that gets
worse, not better, when a whole game is run as a batch.

The fix is to make the default `eval/m1/<possession_id>` and move p0001's existing files under
`eval/m1/p0001/`. Not done here because it churns committed evidence at the end of a session
and deserves its own commit. **Until then, always pass `--eval-dir` explicitly.**

## Constants that were suspect and did **not** need touching

Recorded because "did not move" is a result:

| constant | why it was suspect | what happened |
|---|---|---|
| kit fit's **20 L\* separation floor** (`ur/team.MIN_SEPARATION`) | night footage, different white balance, endzone framing | **cleared.** p0003 separates at **48.2 L\***, *wider* than p0001's 41.8. 1663 sol / 1836 chill / 16 ref / 6 crew / 2 unassigned. |
| **the calibration seed and its feature expectations** | "possibly no soccer centre circle at all" (docs/22) | **cleared, and the premise was wrong.** Measured every 15th frame, p0003 shows the circle on 97 % of frames and the halfway line on 67 %, against p0001's 100 % and 58 %. p0003 is the *richer* clip. |
| **`SIGMA_V_INF`**, `SIGMA_ACCEL`, `DAMP_TAU_S` | sport constants asserted from p0001 | untouched; the tracker produced 14 slots × 555 frames with no phantom or missing slot. Not re-measured — `tools/m4_speed` needs labels p0003 does not have. |
| detector confidence floor (0.25), `MIN_BOX_H`, `MIN_HEIGHT_RATIO` | wider framing, smaller players | untouched. p0003 yields *more* detections per frame (27.7 vs 18.9), not fewer. |
| **`FOOT_UNCERTAINTY_PX = 5.83`** | measured on p0001's 50 hand-labelled feet | untouched, and **unverified on p0003** — checking it needs hand labels this possession does not have. Listed as an open risk, not a cleared one. |

## Constants that are now doing more work than the evidence supports

**`venue.BREESE_STEVENS_NEAR_SIDELINE_Y = -32.75`.** Not changed, but its diagnostic moved:
`residual_yd` is **0.0 on p0001 and 6.0 on p0003**. That number is the distance from the
asserted sideline to the nearest peak the paint map actually found. On p0001 the assertion
lands exactly on measured paint; on p0003 it is six yards from anything. The value is almost
certainly still right — it is the same venue — but on p0003 it is an assumption rather than a
measurement, and every position inherits that. **Watch it; do not average it away.**

| detections | 6801 (18.9/frame) | 15378 (27.7/frame) |

| kit separation | 41.8 L\* | 48.2 L\* |

| median coverage per frame | 10 / 14 | **6 / 14** |

**It runs. It does not run well.** The pipeline completed without a single hand-tuned constant,
and `live-data.js` renders p0003 — but the tracker sees a player on **34.7 %** of slot-frames
against p0001's 67.9 %, and the median frame shows 6 of 14 players rather than 10.

The whole gap traces to one place. 47 % of p0003's detections are refused a field position
because `MIN_CALIB_CONFIDENCE = 0.5` and only 53 % of frames clear it. The detector is finding
*more* people than on p0001; the calibration cannot place them. That is the right behaviour —
AD-1 says a weak frame must down-weight its detections rather than corrupt them — and it means
**the single lever for p0003 is M1, not the tracker and not the detector.**

The most likely next move is the one section 1 left undone: chain the bundle's probe-frame
initialisation outward from the anchor instead of jumping to each from the anchor's pose. That
would let the bundle succeed rather than merely be rejected, and the bundle is worth 0.10 of
mean confidence on p0001.

## Determinism

Two full calibration runs of p0003 — the clip that exercises both the bundled and the anchor
pass — produce a **byte-identical** `calibration.json`
(`sha256 8a104765…4d3ea97f`). Each pass is seeded from `[SEED, pass_index]` rather than
sharing one stream, so the result does not depend on how many passes ran.

## What this costs p0001, and what is outstanding

M1 changed, so p0001's committed calibration is no longer what the current code produces. The
delta was measured rather than assumed:

| | committed | current code |
|---|---|---|
| camera centre | (0.4658, −50.2128, 6.9763) | **identical** |
| frames ≥ 0.5 | 93.9 % | 93.9 % |
| median fit residual | 0.1490 yd | 0.1498 yd |
| venue residual | 0.0 yd | 0.0 yd |

The 0.0008 yd is the per-pass reseeding, not a change of answer. **`work/p0001` has been left
at its committed calibration** so that its detections, tracks and gates stay internally
consistent. Re-running p0001 end to end under the new M1 and re-measuring M4's gates is
**outstanding work**, not done here, and it should move nothing.

**Also outstanding:** step 2 of the M7 brief — the cut detector's threshold and the field-
visibility gate — is **not started**.
